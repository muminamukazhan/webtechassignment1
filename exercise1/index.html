Audrey Hepburn

1929 — 1993 · Actress, Style Icon, Humanitarian

About Audrey Hepburn

Audrey Hepburn was born on May 4, 1929, in Brussels, Belgium, and passed away on January 20, 1993. She became one of Hollywood's most famous actresses in the 1950s and 1960s, starring in films such as Roman Holiday and Breakfast at Tiffany's. She is also remembered as a lasting style icon and for her close work with fashion designer Hubert de Givenchy. In her later years she devoted much of her time to humanitarian work as a UNICEF Goodwill Ambassador.

Career Highlights

  1. 1929 — Born in Brussels, Belgium.
  2. 1953 — Won the Academy Award for Best Actress for Roman Holiday.
  3. 1954 — Won a Tony Award for the play Ondine.
  4. 1961 — Starred in Breakfast at Tiffany's.
  5. 1967 — Nominated for an Academy Award for Wait Until Dark.
  6. 1988 — Became a UNICEF Goodwill Ambassador.

Legacy & Contributions

  • Academy Award and Golden Globe winning actress
  • One of the most famous fashion icons of the 20th century
  • UNICEF Goodwill Ambassador from 1988 until her death
  • Humanitarian missions in Ethiopia and Latin America
  • Received the Presidential Medal of Freedom in 1992
